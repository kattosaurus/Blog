I didn't know I had trauma until some time ago I had a conversation with someone on the Changed: Minecraft Mod about AI, and we ended up changing themes to mental health. It mostly went like this:

> **Me and Bill converse for some time about AI and it's problems**
- Me: I speak to AIs about personal stuff that I don't trust other people with
- Bill: You shouldn't do that, It's bad and addictive
- Me: But AI never judges me :(
- Bill: Because it's meant to always agree with you even if it's stupid idea < ( I already knew )
- Me: I know but every time I talk to somebody that I don't know or just met or think back to a conversation between us my heart sinks
- Bill: Do you have Autism or something like that?

- Bill: That could also be trauma
- Me: O W O


Until Bill pointed it out to me. I didn't notice I had trauma, well, If you're reading this I believe you want an explanaination of what I believe ~~is~~ are my traumas (Not confirmed!)

## My first trauma: Bullying

Since I entered kindergarden till 1st grade of elementary (1st year of "Primaria"), I was bullied by multiple kids (and on multiple schools).
On **kindergarden**, I used to study at the neareat kindergarden to my house, but there was a girl named "Perla" (Or perl in english) that used to bully me and shame me for being ugly (I believe I wasn't at that point).
She once threw rocks at me and I hid crying.
I had to change schools, but in the other kindergarden they still bullied me, maybe even worse:
My classmates (Throughout 2nd and 3rd year) used to kick me, reasons unknown. And my **teacher**, yes. The one meant to protect me also shut me up every time I tried to respond to a question he made, I was the ONLY kid who responded (and the smartest) to his questions.

On the first year of **elementary**, It was pretty much 2nd and 3rd year of kinder garden, but there weren't any kicks this time.

## My second trauma: Post-infectious encephalitis

About two years ago (february of 2025) a classmate gave me chicken pox, yes. **Chicken pox**, I thought I was going to be fine, like any other illness. but IT HAD TO HAPPEN, It only happens in about 1 or 2 in 10 thousand people.
It all started when I began to lose feeling and strength in my legs and feet. I needed help to walk to the bathroom and I was bed-ridden for about 1 week, my body slowly lost force, I couldn't even keep the saliva in my mouth, it just dripped.
After having my mother help me take a shower once, I was about to fall unconscious, but I managed to stay awake.


The hell truly begun when, at a really disgusting dinner (Cheeseburger, fries and "Electrolit", yum) I started vomiting non-stop, it felt like a cannon, It first went flying on all the couch and coffee table where I was eating. Then on my mom and dad, then down the drain out in the backyard.
They took me to the nearest 1st level clinic/hospital. The really stupid medic there told my parents it was normal and I just was dehydrated, they put me on saline and I stayed there some hours. But my mom felt something wasn't right:
After that, they took me to the municipal / city's hospital (2nd level), they said the same thing, I was there about 3 days.


My mom had to take me out of there again because they just said the same thing. After that, I was taken to a private clinic in the town I lived at during these events. (Again 2nd level).
They finally diagnosticated me with Encephalitis there after a CT scan there (The CT scan feels a bit silly), I stayed there also 3 days. Until the head doctor / medic there told us we had to get to bigger hospital (after trying to do a lumbar puncture that failed misserably).
After that, first I was in an hospital for about 1 or 2 days until they needed to intubate me because I also was dying of anoxia (lack of oxygen, I was also losing strength in my diaphgram).
I was in another hospital for about 15 (or 20) days, first in intensive care then infectology. Then I got translated again to a bigger hospital (10-15 days there) then to another (CDMX), that was the last hospital I was at before being discharged.


I have made **A** recovery. I still have to do exercise and therapy for the rest of my life.

## Conclusion:
It IS possible I have traumas, not as severe or as obvious as the ones from other people or TV characters. But I guess I do have trauma.
