> # Dr. Murray Arthur Barr: "Katto"
> 
> **Dr. Murray Arthur Barr**, known as **Dr. B**, and later as ***Katto***, was a head scientist at the Biological Studies Facility. He is now a white-grey **latex katto**.
> 
> ## Background
> 
> - **Age:** 34, transfurred at 32
> - **Studied:** Microbiology, and Medicine with a specialty in Pathology
> - **Career:** Worked at TSC for two years, recruited when the pale began
> 
> At the Biological Studies Facility he led research on latex. His goal was a way to transfur humans safely, keeping their consciousness intact.
> 
> ## The Incident
> 
> One night, working overtime, he skipped a single security protocol and was transfurred. As a last resort, he used his own treatment on himself. He didn't know if it would kill him.
> 
> It worked. He stayed all night in the lab, waiting for help. When someone finally came, they called security, believing his experiment had failed and he was just another mindless latex creature. He begged them not to take him, insisting he was Dr. Barr. They thought he was lying.
> 
> He was locked in a room built for test subjects. The tests that followed were like torture. Eventually he had enough. As an employee, he knew the researchers' and guards' habits and every slip they made.
> 
> ## The Breach
> 
> On **15 August**, one year to the day after EXP-012 was contained, he broke out and returned the torture in a permanent way. He massacred the entire facility. No one was left alive but him.
> 
> He felt satisfied, even happy, until he thought back on what he'd done. He had killed his friends. He screamed and cried, and swore never to kill again, to be a pacifist. He left the facility and built himself a bunker.
> 
> ## Form and personality
> 
> - **Species:** White latex beast
> - **Variant:** White-grey tiger, made by himself
> - He aims for neutrality, and tries not to be seen as bad or evil by anyone.
> - He still wears the jacket he wore as a human, and pants he stole from a guard.
> 
> ## Relationship with EXP-012
> 
> Dr. B was the researcher who stood outside her window, talking with her and sneaking her paint, brushes, and paper. He even got her an apron by bargaining with the other researchers. He was present at her sealing, and cried.
> 
> Years later, she still knows him by the jacket he always wore at her window.
> 
> > *"Wait, I recognize you! You're Dr. B! I would never forget you."*
> 
> > *"I never treated any experiment wrongly, I treated them as any human being. But my mind silenced how bad the others treated them."*
> 
> ## Journal
> 
> His journal was hidden in his old jacket. The pages before his transfur were ripped out in a hurry, and dry latex and ash cover what remains. Its last entry is from the day of the breach.
